Slip Scanner

Scan on your phone or iPad (Adobe Scan works well), then load the finished PDFs or photos here. Each file becomes one invoice. Claude reads the figures, you check them, and approved invoices are filed into Invoices_slip and added to the receipt register.

Load your scans

Select as many files as you like. On iPad or iPhone the Files picker shows Adobe Scan documents saved to iCloud or On My Device. You can also drag files onto this box. A PDF with several pages stays one invoice, and can be split into one invoice per page.

Check what Claude read from each invoice. Fix anything that is wrong, then approve it. Only approved invoices are filed.
